Oxford Board Games Society

We are the University of Oxford's society for board games. Whether you're into quick card games, long strategy games, party games, or just fancy trying something new, you're very welcome to come along.

About us

Oxford Board Games Society brings together people from across the University to play board and card games. You don't need to be an experienced player, and there's no need to bring your own games.

Have a look at our termcard for upcoming events, or join the mailing list to hear about what we're doing.
